What You'll Learn

By the end of this course

  • How to test a long-lived automation or bot account’s authorization scope and SoD exposure with the same discipline applied to any human account.
  • How to test an AI/ML model’s current behavior against current data, rather than trusting its original validation.
  • How to independently verify AI-assisted tooling—log review summaries, monitoring dashboards, generated code—against the raw evidence it claims to represent.
  • How to extend SoD and access testing beyond SAP itself to identity-provider group management and cross-system, post-merger populations.
  • How to recognize when a finding that looks single-domain is actually a compound, multi-domain control gap.
