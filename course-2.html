Scope & Objective

What this course is built around

Setting: a stable manufacturing company with a mature-looking, already-documented control environment.

Where the Foundation course teaches what to test, this course teaches how a control that looks fine on paper can still be broken in practice. Every one of its 22 scenarios is built around a control that was documented, approved, and signed off — and still didn't operate as intended. Its objective is judgment: training a consultant to test operating effectiveness, not just confirm a control's existence.

What You'll Learn

By the end of this course

  • How to test whether a signed-off mitigating control is actually being reviewed, not just re-signed.
  • How to catch a standing Firefighter ID, a role redesign that reintroduces a conflict, or a Fiori tile that grants more than its label suggests.
  • How to independently reperform a management estimate instead of accepting a rolled-forward figure.
  • How to test whether a GRC alert or risk-control linkage is actually monitored, not just configured.
  • How to spot the specific gap between “the control exists” and “the control operates” across all four domains.
