Scope & Objective

What this course is built around

Setting: a newly public company running SAP S/4HANA and SAP GRC Access Control.

This is the foundation course in the series. It covers the full width of a standard SOX/ITGC and GRC engagement at a single, stable company that recently went public — the baseline every other course in the series builds on. Its objective is breadth: one clean, worked example of how to test each major category of SAP control, so a trainee finishes with a complete mental map of what SOX/ITGC testing covers before specializing further.

What You'll Learn

By the end of this course

  • How to run and interpret Access Risk Analysis (GRAC_ARA) to test segregation of duties at both role and user level.
  • How to test change management controls end-to-end in STMS, from transport approval through import.
  • How to trace a journal entry, reconciliation, or estimate back to its underlying source data in FB03 and FBL3N.
  • How to confirm whether GRC Process Control and Risk Management modules are actually monitoring what they claim to.
  • How to test governance and disclosure controls the way an audit committee would want them tested.
  • How to recognize data privacy and software licensing exposure inside a routine SAP environment.
