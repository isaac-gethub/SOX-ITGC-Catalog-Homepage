Scope & Objective

What this course is built around

Setting: a company mid-way through a merger integration, running two organizations’ SAP environments, org charts, and control populations at once.

This course's objective is complexity at scale: testing controls in an environment where nothing sits still. Every scenario is shaped by the specific ways a merger or acquisition breaks assumptions that hold in a single, stable entity — new org structures, shared services spanning legal entities, inherited codebases, consolidation logic, and platform migrations. It trains a consultant to test whether controls genuinely cover the combined organization, not just the entity they were originally designed for.

What You'll Learn

By the end of this course

  • How to test SoD risk created purely by an org-chart change, with no role redesign involved.
  • How to test change management and vendor reliance across a shared-services environment serving multiple legal entities.
  • How to reconcile intercompany eliminations transaction-by-transaction — not at the summary level — and catch legacy-entity-coded transactions that silently fail to eliminate.
  • How to confirm a parent-level control’s tested population actually includes every newly consolidated subsidiary.
  • How to test whether a GRC ruleset was updated to recognize the authorization objects a system migration introduced.
